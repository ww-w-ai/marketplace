Spacing is a hard requirement. State it in every prompt:
- Leave generous, even gaps between every component: title, subline, diagram, cards, labels. Name the minimum gap in px (at least 40px between blocks, at least 24px between sibling cards).
- Use consistent inner padding (at least 32px) inside every card and panel.
- Keep equal gaps between peer elements and at least 80px from slide edges. No component touches or crowds another, and no label sits against its shape.
- When the layout feels dense, remove or shrink content before tightening gaps.
- After generation, check the gaps in the image. If any pair of components is tight, regenerate with the tight pair named.

Create a complete 16:9 slide design. Request 1920×1080. Include the supplied text in the first composition. Use authentic screenshots as references. Do not invent evidence or numeric results. Match the approved reference style.

After approval, remove only slide typography from this image. Preserve artwork, spacing, and composition. Keep authentic screenshot text intact. The final slide will restore headings, body copy, labels, and page numbers in editable HTML.

Record the submitted prompt, selected output path, actual pixel dimensions, and review notes in the project. The source template does not invoke image generation automatically.
